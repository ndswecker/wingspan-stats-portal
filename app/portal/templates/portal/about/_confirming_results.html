Confirming Game Results

Keep your game history accurate.

When a friend records a game you played together, your results are added to your account automatically. However, you'll need to confirm your score before that result is included in the site's statistics and analysis.

How to Confirm a Result

  1. Navigate to Account from the main menu.
  2. Find the Pending Confirmation section. This lists games awaiting your approval.
  3. Select View Game to review the recorded results.
  4. If your result is correct, select Confirm My Result.
Good to know: Games become fully confirmed once every participating player has confirmed their own result. Confirmed results contribute to the site's statistics and analysis.
