Recording Games

Every game deserves a place in your history.

Whether you're playing solo or competing with friends, Wingspan Stats Portal makes it easy to record your games and build a lasting history of your scores. Each game you record contributes to your growing collection of Wingspan results.

Start from My Games

Select My Games from the main navigation to access your personal game dashboard. From here, select Add New Game to record a completed Wingspan game.

Enter Your Game Results

Recording a game takes just a few steps:

  1. Choose the date. Select when your game was played. The date defaults to today, but you can change it if needed.
  2. Select the game mode. Indicate whether you played solo or with other human players.
  3. Enter player scores. Select yourself or friends who participated and enter each player's final score.
  4. Add additional players. Use the Add Another Player button to include more participants, up to five human players. Only your friends can be added to a game. You can also record turn order if you wish.
  5. Save the game. Your completed game is recorded in Wingspan Stats Portal.

A note about player results

Your own results are automatically confirmed when you record a game. Friends you include must confirm their results before the game is fully verified. We'll explain how that works in the Confirming Results section.
