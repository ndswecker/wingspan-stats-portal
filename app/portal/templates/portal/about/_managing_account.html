Managing Your Account

Your Wingspan identity, your achievements, your privacy.

Your Account page brings together your player information, privacy preferences, achievements, and game notifications. It's your personal home for managing how you participate in Wingspan Stats Portal.

Your Player Profile

Select Account from the main navigation to view your account information, including your username, email address, player name, and unique player handle.

Your player handle helps identify you within the Wingspan Stats Portal community.

You're in Control of Your Privacy

Not everyone wants their gaming statistics shared with the entire community. That's why Wingspan Stats Portal lets you choose who can view your player profile.

Private Friends Only

Only you and your friends can view your player statistics and confirmed game results.

Public Community Visibility

Any logged-in user can view your confirmed game results and statistics. Your name and handle can also appear on the public leaderboard.

You can change your visibility whenever you like. Simply select or clear Make my player profile public and save your privacy settings.

Good to know: Changing your profile visibility does not affect your friendships or who can record games on your behalf.

Your Achievements

Your Account page also displays achievements you've earned while playing Wingspan.

From reaching impressive scores to memorable victories (and occasionally spectacular defeats), achievements recognize milestones throughout your playing history.

Each achievement includes a description of what you've accomplished and how many times you've earned it.
