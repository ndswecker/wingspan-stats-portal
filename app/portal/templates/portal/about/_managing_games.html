Managing Games

Your game history, organized and easy to manage.

Wingspan Stats Portal keeps your recorded games together in one place. Find previous games, review their results, and correct mistakes when necessary. You can manage games that you participated in.

Find Your Games

Select My Games from the main navigation to browse your playing history. You can filter your games by game type or date range to find a particular session.

Each game displays your score, the participating players, and its confirmation status. Select the game's date to open its details.

Review Game Details

Each recorded game has its own details page. Here you can review when the game was played, who participated, everyone's scores, and the order in which players took their turns, if recorded.

You can also see which players have confirmed their results. We'll explain this process further in the Confirming Results section.

Correct a Game

Made a mistake when entering a game? If you participated in the game, you can select Edit Game from its details page to make corrections.

You can update the game date, game type, player scores, and turn order. You can also add or remove participants when correcting an existing game.

Select Save Changes when you're finished. Changes to player results may require those players to confirm their updated scores.

Delete an Incorrect Game

If a game was entered by mistake or should no longer be part of your history, you can delete it from the Edit Game page.

Important: Deleting a game permanently removes its player results and any associated achievement awards. This action cannot be undone through the application.
